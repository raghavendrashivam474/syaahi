# Syaahi Studio — Product Identity

## 1. Product Name
**Syaahi Studio** (or simply **Studio** within the Syaahi application context).

## 2. Product Category
**Creative Writing Environment / Poetry Composition Workspace**

## 3. Product Positioning
Syaahi Studio is an integrated writing workspace embedded inside the Syaahi ecosystem. It enables poets and creative writers to capture thoughts, explore words, experiment with poetic forms, refine drafts, and preserve unfinished ideas in a distraction-free, privacy-conscious canvas.

It is **not** a conventional word processor (like MS Word or Google Docs), nor is it an automated AI poem generator.

## 4. Product Philosophy
> **AI assists. The writer creates.**

Core principles:
1. **Creativity before automation:** Technology exists to enhance human imagination, not to replace the author's voice or automate composition.
2. **Simplicity before unnecessary complexity:** Tools must remain unobtrusive. Writing requires focus, calm, and clarity.
3. **Expression before engagement metrics:** Studio serves the writer's internal creative process, not algorithms or social gratification loops.
4. **Privacy and ownership of personal writing:** Drafts, fragments, and unpolished lines belong strictly to the writer until explicitly published.
5. **Low-cost engineering without compromising quality:** Built local-first, avoiding expensive API models or recurring cloud operational burdens.
6. **Non-interruptive tooling:** Suggestions, rhymes, and dictionary explorations are contextual and available on demand, never intrusive or auto-applying.

## 5. Tagline (Proposed)
> *"Where words find their form."*
